Head-to-head record between two group members, per game or overall.

```jsx
<HeadToHead left="Jasper" right="Mara" leftWins={4} rightWins={7} draws={1} />
```

- Left is always `--series-1`, right `--series-2`, draws `--ink-faint`. Do not colour by who is winning.
- Put it in a `Card` under a `SectionLabel` reading "Onderling", with the two players chosen above it.
